## What SaaS teams are up against

Early-stage software companies in Hyderabad face a familiar squeeze. Investors want growth, customers want features, and the first enterprise prospect arrives with a 200-question security questionnaire and a request for a penetration test report and SOC 2 or ISO 27001 evidence. Hiring a full security team is out of reach, and the product was built quickly, often by a small team making sensible but undocumented decisions.

## A typical sequence

Many startups start with an architecture and security review, fix the top findings, run a penetration test, then begin SOC 2 Type I to unblock deals while collecting evidence for Type II. We sequence the work so each step makes the next cheaper, and we tell you plainly when something can wait.

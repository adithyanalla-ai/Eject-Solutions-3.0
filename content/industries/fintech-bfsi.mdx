## The stakes are higher here

Financial services applications hold money, identity documents and credit data, which makes them constant targets. Regulators and partner banks expect regular security testing, strong logging and fast incident reporting. Customers abandon apps at the first hint of fraud. Speed to market still matters, which creates tension between shipping and assurance.

## How we help

**Test what attackers actually try.** In [VAPT](/services/vapt-penetration-testing) for lending, wallet, trading and insurance apps we focus on account takeover, broken authorisation between customers, transaction tampering, KYC document handling, API abuse and mobile app weaknesses such as insecure storage and weak pinning.

**Detect and respond.** [Managed threat monitoring](/services/managed-threat-monitoring-incident-response) covers identity, cloud and application logs, with runbooks that include regulatory and partner notification timelines such as CERT-In's six-hour reporting direction.

**Build with control in mind.** Our [engineering team](/services/custom-software-development) designs auditable workflows, role separation, maker-checker patterns, immutable logs and resilient integrations with banks and payment aggregators.

**Prepare evidence.** [ISO 27001, SOC 2 and DPDP](/services/compliance-readiness-iso-27001-soc-2-dpdp) programmes give auditors, partners and enterprise clients a consistent set of controls and evidence.

**Use AI carefully.** Customer-service assistants and document-processing agents need strict guardrails around personal and financial data, so we pair delivery with [AI security testing](/services/ai-security-governance).

## Working style

We agree rules of engagement, data handling and communication channels before testing starts. Reports map findings to the control language your auditors use, and we support retesting before partner or regulator deadlines.

## Scale, speed and global standards

Hyderabad hosts a dense cluster of IT services companies and global capability centres. Their challenges are rarely about whether talent exists; they are about hiring quickly for specific skills, applying global security and AI policies locally, and enabling large teams to adopt new tools without creating new risks.

## How we help

**Targeted hiring and augmentation.** [Recruitment and staffing](/services/ai-recruitment-it-staffing) for specialist roles in cloud, security, data and AI, with practitioner screening and shortlists that explain themselves.

**Delivery capacity.** [Engineering support](/services/custom-software-development) for internal platforms, automation and integrations when your own backlog outgrows your team.

**Safe AI enablement.** Global parents often publish AI policies that local teams struggle to apply. We provide [AI governance and security testing](/services/ai-security-governance) that turn policy into practical controls: approved tools, data classification rules, red-teaming of internal assistants and monitoring.

**Skills at scale.** [Training programmes](/services/ai-upskilling-training-internships) in AI-assisted development, secure coding and safe use of generative tools, with practical exercises in your environment.

**Evidence for audits.** [Compliance support](/services/compliance-readiness-iso-27001-soc-2-dpdp) for ISO 27001, SOC 2 and DPDP, aligned with parent-company assessments.

## What we do not claim

We are a focused team, not a thousand-person outsourcer. We will say clearly when a requirement exceeds our capacity and suggest alternatives, which tends to build more trust than overselling.

## Working in Hyderabad

Proximity helps with onsite workshops, interview days and rapid ramp-up. Many of our conversations start with a single team's problem and grow from there.

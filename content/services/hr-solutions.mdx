## The problem: HR run on spreadsheets, chats and goodwill

As a company grows past a few dozen people, HR work quietly turns into a second job for managers. Leave balances live in a spreadsheet, attendance comes from three sources, offer letters are edited by hand, payroll depends on one person who knows the formulas, and candidates are tracked in an inbox. Mistakes are small at first, a wrong deduction, a missed probation review, a lost CV. Then they compound into disputes, audit findings and good people leaving because the basics were unreliable.

HR technology exists to remove that fragility. The goal is not to digitise paperwork for its own sake, but to make the common processes fast, consistent and auditable, so HR can spend time on people instead of reconciliation.

## What we deliver

### HRMS: the system of record for people

We build and integrate human resource management systems covering the employee lifecycle:

- **Employee records and documents** with role-based access and an audit trail.
- **Onboarding and offboarding workflows** with checklists, e-signatures, asset tracking and exit clearances.
- **Leave, attendance and shifts** with policy rules, holiday calendars per location, regularisation requests and approvals.
- **Performance and goals:** review cycles, goal tracking, feedback and calibration, designed around how your managers actually work.
- **Employee self-service** on web and mobile, so people can view payslips, apply for leave and download letters without raising a ticket.
- **Reports and analytics** on headcount, attrition, leave liability and attendance patterns.

### ATS: applicant tracking and talent acquisition

An applicant tracking system gives structure to hiring: job posting, application capture, pipeline stages, interview scheduling, scorecards, offer approvals and a talent pool you can search later. We build intelligent features where they help, such as resume parsing, duplicate detection, skills-based search and summaries against a role scorecard, always with transparent criteria and human decisions. See our [AI-assisted recruitment and IT staffing](/services/ai-recruitment-it-staffing) service for how we use these tools in practice.

### Payroll automation

Payroll is where small errors become expensive. Our payroll infrastructure takes attendance and leave as inputs, applies salary structures, variable pay and reimbursements, computes statutory components such as provident fund, ESI, professional tax and TDS on salary according to configurable rules, generates payslips, bank disbursement files and journal entries for accounting, and keeps a full audit trail of every change. Statutory rules vary by state and change over time, so we make them data, not code, and we build in parallel-run reports so you can compare against your current process before cutting over.

## Our approach

1. **Map the processes first.** We sit with HR, finance and a few managers and write down how work actually flows, including the exceptions that spreadsheets quietly absorb.
2. **Decide build, buy or integrate.** If a mainstream product fits, we help you choose and configure it. If not, we build a focused system around your rules.
3. **Phase the rollout.** Employee records first, then leave and attendance, then payroll, with each phase delivering value and a clean data migration.
4. **Design for trust.** Employees will only use the system if payslips are right and requests are answered. Accuracy, clear status messages and fast support matter more than features.
5. **Secure by default.** Salary and identity data get the strongest protection in the system.

## Security, privacy and compliance

HR systems hold some of the most sensitive data a company has. We apply least-privilege roles, separate duties between HR and payroll, mask sensitive fields by default, log every access to salary and identity data, encrypt data in transit and at rest and define retention and deletion rules. Under India's DPDP Act, employee data needs clear notices and a lawful basis, which we design into the workflows, though final interpretation belongs with your legal counsel. Our [compliance readiness](/services/compliance-readiness-iso-27001-soc-2-dpdp) team can include the HR system in ISO 27001 or SOC 2 scope, and [penetration testing](/services/vapt-penetration-testing) is available before go-live.

## The problem: compliance as a stalled deal

For most growing companies compliance arrives as a deadline from a customer: "send us your ISO 27001 certificate or SOC 2 report before we sign." Or it arrives as a law: India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 puts duties on any business handling personal data. Without a plan, teams burn weeks copying policy templates nobody follows, then scramble to collect screenshots the week before the audit.

Good compliance work does the reverse. It makes your real practices explicit, closes real gaps and produces evidence as a by-product of normal work.

## What we cover

### ISO 27001

We help you scope and implement an information security management system (ISMS): context and scope, leadership commitment, risk assessment and treatment, the Statement of Applicability against the Annex A controls of ISO/IEC 27001:2022, policies and procedures, internal audit and management review. We prepare you for the stage 1 and stage 2 audits run by an accredited certification body.

### SOC 2

We help you decide scope and Trust Services Criteria (security is mandatory; availability, confidentiality, processing integrity and privacy are optional), design controls, set up evidence collection and run a readiness assessment before your CPA firm's audit. We cover both Type I and Type II.

### DPDP Act readiness

We map where personal data lives, why you collect it and who touches it, then build what the Act expects of you: notice and consent, purpose limitation, processes for access, correction and erasure requests, a grievance channel, breach response, vendor and processor controls, children's data rules where relevant, and retention schedules. We track the DPDP Rules and their phased commencement, and we flag where you should seek legal confirmation, because we are not a law firm.

## Our approach

1. **Gap assessment.** Interviews, document review and technical checks against the chosen framework, producing a plain-language gap report with effort estimates.
2. **Roadmap and ownership.** Every control gets an owner and a date, and the engineering asks are grouped so they fit into sprints.
3. **Build the programme.** Risk register, policies, procedures, training, vendor management and incident response, written for your size and tools.
4. **Technical controls.** Hardening, access reviews, logging, backup and recovery tests, vulnerability management and secure development practices, supported by our [VAPT](/services/vapt-penetration-testing) and [monitoring](/services/managed-threat-monitoring-incident-response) services where needed.
5. **Evidence and internal audit.** Automated evidence collection where possible, a mock audit and corrective actions.
6. **Audit support.** Auditor selection guidance, walkthrough preparation and responses to queries.

## What it takes from your side

Compliance programmes stall when nobody owns them. We ask for a named internal sponsor with authority, a part-time programme owner, and short, scheduled slots with engineering, HR and operations leads. In a typical mid-sized company that is a few hours a week per person during the heavy phases. We keep meetings short, prepare drafts so people react instead of writing from scratch, and give you a simple tracker showing what is done, what is blocked and what is next.

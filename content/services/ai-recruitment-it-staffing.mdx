## The problem: slow, noisy hiring in a competitive market

Hyderabad is one of India's busiest technology hiring markets. Good engineers receive several messages a week, strong candidates take multiple offers, and hiring managers lose days reading CVs that do not match the role. Meanwhile generic recruiters forward keyword-matched profiles without understanding the work, and AI tools promise to automate everything but often produce confident-sounding shortlists nobody can explain.

We combine both worlds: technology to remove tedious work, and practitioners to judge what matters.

## Our approach: AI for the admin, people for the judgement

**Intake that goes beyond a job description.** We talk to the hiring manager about the work itself: the systems, the first ninety days, the team, what has failed before. From that we write a skills-based scorecard before we search.

**Sourcing with assistance.** AI tools help us search across professional networks, our own talent pool and referrals, de-duplicate candidates, and draft personalised outreach that mentions the actual role. This is where automation saves the most time.

**Screening by practitioners.** Screening is not keyword matching. Our technical recruiters, backed by engineers from our delivery teams for senior and specialist roles, run structured conversations and, where appropriate, short practical exercises. AI summarises CVs against the scorecard to speed reading, but a person verifies every claim that matters.

**Transparent shortlists.** Each candidate you receive comes with a short note: what they have done, how it maps to your scorecard, where the gaps are and what they want from their next role. You see the reasoning, not just a score.

**Candidate care.** We tell candidates the truth about role, salary band and client type, keep them updated and follow up after joining. Reputation in a city like Hyderabad is built one candidate at a time.

## Engagement models

- **Permanent placement:** a full search for roles you intend to keep, billed on successful placement.
- **Contract staffing:** engineers or testers you need quickly for a project, engaged for a fixed duration.
- **Contract-to-hire:** a trial period before conversion, useful when team fit is the main uncertainty.
- **Dedicated team augmentation:** a group of engineers working with your team, optionally combined with our [software engineering](/services/custom-software-development) practice.
- **Security and AI talent searches:** specialist hiring for penetration testers, SOC analysts, cloud security engineers and AI/ML practitioners, drawing on our own [security](/services/vapt-penetration-testing) and [AI](/services/ai-agents-rag-assistants) teams for assessment.

## Deliverables

1. Role scorecard and sourcing plan agreed with you.
2. Weekly pipeline update: candidates sourced, screened, shortlisted and the reasons for decisions.
3. Shortlist packs with candidate summaries and assessment notes.
4. Interview scheduling and feedback collection.
5. Offer support: compensation benchmarks, negotiation and notice-period management.
6. Post-joining check-ins at agreed intervals.

## Process

1. **Brief:** role, team, stack, budget and timeline.
2. **Scorecard:** must-haves, nice-to-haves and signals we will test.
3. **Source and screen:** outreach, structured screening and practical checks.
4. **Shortlist:** three to five candidates with notes, not a pile of CVs.
5. **Interview and decide:** we coordinate and gather structured feedback.
6. **Offer and onboard:** negotiate, track notice periods and support the first weeks.

## Responsible use of AI in hiring

AI recruitment tools can make hiring faster, but they carry real risks: biased training data, opaque ranking, privacy exposure and candidates who feel processed rather than considered. Our rules are simple. Automated tools may assist but never decide. We use skills-based criteria and avoid inferring protected characteristics. We keep candidate data only for the purpose they agreed to, under our [privacy policy](/privacy), and we delete it on request. If you want to build or buy your own AI recruiting workflow, we can also help you design it with human oversight, using our [AI governance](/services/ai-security-governance) approach.

## What we ask of you

Good hiring is a partnership. We ask hiring managers to join a thirty-minute intake call, give feedback on shortlists within two working days, and keep interview panels consistent so candidates are compared fairly. In return we commit to honest timelines, to telling you early when a brief is unrealistic for the market, and to never sending a candidate to you without their knowledge and consent. If a role is stuck, we will tell you why, whether that is compensation, location, stack or process, instead of sending more profiles and hoping.

## The problem: manual marketing that does not scale

Growing businesses often have the same marketing pattern. Leads arrive through a form, WhatsApp, email and events, and land in different places. Someone follows up when they remember. Newsletters go out when there is time. Reports are assembled by hand at month end. Content creation depends on one person with a free afternoon. The result is slow responses, uneven nurturing and no clear picture of what works.

AI makes some of this much cheaper, but it also makes it easy to produce a lot of mediocre output quickly. The goal is not more content. It is a reliable system that responds fast, learns what works and keeps a human in charge of the message.

## Our approach

We design marketing automation as a small, observable system, not a pile of integrations.

1. **Map the funnel.** From first touch to closed customer, with the real handoffs, delays and drop-offs.
2. **Fix the data first.** One source of truth in your CRM, consistent fields, de-duplication and consent records. Automation on top of messy data just automates the mess.
3. **Automate the repeatable steps.** Routing, enrichment, reminders, sequences, and reporting.
4. **Add AI where it helps.** Lead qualification summaries, reply drafts for sales to approve, content briefs and repurposing, call-note summaries and anomaly flags in reports.
5. **Keep review points.** Anything customer-facing and new is approved by a person. Anything routine and low-risk runs automatically with sampling and monitoring.
6. **Measure and iterate.** A simple dashboard, a monthly review and a backlog of experiments.

## What we build

- **Lead capture and routing:** forms, chat and WhatsApp entry points feeding one CRM, with instant acknowledgement and assignment rules.
- **Nurture sequences:** email and WhatsApp journeys based on behaviour and stage, with opt-in and easy opt-out.
- **Sales assist:** AI-generated lead summaries, suggested replies and follow-up reminders inside your CRM.
- **Content operations:** editorial calendar, research briefs, draft generation with human editing, repurposing a long guide into posts, emails and short summaries, and a review checklist covering facts, sources and originality.
- **SEO support:** keyword research, internal linking suggestions, content refresh alerts and technical checks, without publishing unreviewed machine text.
- **Reporting:** weekly dashboards joining ad spend, website analytics and CRM outcomes so you see cost per qualified lead, not just clicks.

## Guardrails we insist on

- No purchased lists and no unsolicited bulk messaging.
- No fabricated testimonials, statistics or case studies in generated copy.
- Human approval for new messages, claims and anything involving pricing or legal promises.
- Clear disclosure when a customer is talking to an automated assistant.
- Customer data kept inside systems you control, with access limited by role.

## A realistic first ninety days

In the first month we map the funnel, clean CRM data and set up lead capture and routing, which usually shortens response times immediately. In the second month we launch one or two nurture sequences and a weekly dashboard, and start the editorial calendar with a review workflow. In the third month we add AI-assisted sales summaries and content repurposing, then review the numbers together and decide what to expand. We resist launching ten automations at once, because you cannot learn from ten changes at the same time.
